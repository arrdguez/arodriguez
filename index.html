01 — About

A scientist who ships.

I hold a PhD in Advanced Technologies and spent years in the lab — spectroscopy, magnetometry and computational materials science. That training gave me a permanent taste for rigor: model it, measure it, and don't believe a number until it survives a second look.

Today I design APIs at BBVA, working across data science, macroeconomics and blockchain. I move between two languages — the language of physics and the language of production software — and enjoy translating one into the other.
